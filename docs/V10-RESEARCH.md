the older, softer lighting, "with a much better accent"); the halls, corridors and lobby feel empty; the diamond wall in the corridor
"looks shit"; the lobby is too big and needs filling (lighting, paintings, pillars, sofas, a fountain); escalator and lift as one
area behind the ticket check; reception, concessions and the café more defined, with sounds; restrooms recognisable at a glance;
an unequal entrance door; a better exterior. Every decision below cites what it comes from.

## What it changes

**Lighting.** Nothing in these photos has hard scallops of light on the walls. They are lit by continuous lines (coves, LED lines in
the ceiling, slots), glowing objects (pendants, lit letters, display cases) and soft wash. The floors are glossy but not white-hot.
So: (a) the engine compresses the baked light's hot spots and lifts its shadows (softer, the older look), and the screen's light on a
hall is lower (it read as "too much bounce"); (b) accents are emissive lines and objects (coves, pendant clusters, lit letters,
display cases), which cost nothing to light.

**Corridor.** The diamond cladding goes. The halls' portals become fluted timber with a brass reveal and a warm light slot (sources 2,
6, 8); the corridor gets warm white coves instead of red, a slatted ceiling feature, benches and plants between the posters.

**Lobby.** It is too big and too bare, so it gets rooms within the room (sources 1, 7, 9): a fountain on the axis between the doors and
the box office, pillars with brass rings and uplights, a lounge island under a pendant cloud, rugs, standees of the films on show, art
on the long walls, and a café counter beside the concessions with an espresso machine (its own sounds: the grinder, steam, cups).

**Escalator and lift, one area.** A glass screen joins the ticket gate to the lift, so both are inside one checked zone with its own
floor inlay and a lit sign over the gate (source 11 for the sign).

**Restrooms.** Two-metre pictograms beside each entrance and a dark wayfinding sign with pictograms (sources 10, 11).

**Entrance.** The doors sit on the canopy's axis with equal piers on both sides (the user's photo of 2026-11-07).
